# Spoonful: app description

## Short description (Play Store, 80 characters)

Recipes, nutrition, shopping lists and barcode scanning in one app.

## Full description (Play Store / website)

Spoonful keeps recipes, nutrition, shopping and household lists in one app.

### Recipes

The library holds recipes with photos, preparation time, difficulty and nutrition values. Search by dish or ingredient and filter by cuisine, meal type, total time, calories and diet. A recipe opens as a step by step guide with timers.

### AI Chef

Describe what is in your fridge or what you feel like eating. The AI Chef writes a recipe around your ingredients, your time and your preferences. It can also suggest a substitute for a missing ingredient or shorten an existing recipe.

### Tracker

Log meals by hand, from a recipe or from a scanned product. Spoonful counts calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat per day and per week, against a goal calculated from your body data. There is a weekly report, a water goal and a weight trend.

### Barcode scanning

Point the camera at a barcode. Spoonful reads the code, finds the product and shows its values per 100 grams. Choose a portion, 100 grams, the whole package or your own amount.

### Shopping

Turn a recipe into a shopping list, add items by hand and group the list by aisle or course. Add prices to see the total. A household invite code shares one list between devices.

### Saved recipes

Save recipes into collections, add personal notes and keep your own versions next to the original.

### Community

Publish your own recipes, rate and comment on other recipes, repost what you like and follow other cooks.

### Languages and themes

The interface, recipes and notifications are translated, including right to left layout for Arabic. The app follows the device language on first start and can be changed at any time. Light and dark themes are available.

### Offline

Lists you opened before stay readable without a connection. Changes made offline are sent when the connection returns.

### Data

The app has no advertising. Camera access is used for photos of dishes and for barcodes. Photos are chosen through the system picker. You can export your data as a file and delete your account in the app.

### System requirements

Android 7.0 or newer. An internet connection is recommended for the library, community and AI features. Saved recipes stay available offline.
