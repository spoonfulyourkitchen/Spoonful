# Spoonful v3.5.3: a shopping list that works offline, drafts, and plain errors

Spoonful 3.5.3 rebuilds the shopping list without a server, keeps unfinished forms and filters as drafts, explains failures in normal words with a retry button, puts the offline label next to the page title, and brings the new app icon.

## Highlights

### Shopping list without the server
Adding, checking, deleting and clearing now happen on the device. The list opens instantly, keeps its order, shows a progress bar and lets you undo a delete for five seconds. The same item is never listed twice.

### Drafts that come back
The recipe form saves what you typed, including times, nutrition, diet, ingredients and steps. Leave the screen, come back, and the draft is there with a discard option. Saved recipes also remember the search and the selected shelf, and the shopping input keeps its unfinished text.

### Errors in plain language
When a library page or the random recipe fails, you get a short explanation and a retry button instead of a half loaded list. The crash screen no longer shows the raw exception, and a failed save keeps your draft.

### Offline next to the title
Every screen with a page header shows an offline label beside the title. The old pending counter is gone.

### Cleaner AI Chef
The swipe hints under the carousel are removed. The page dots still show the three modes.

### New icon
The launcher icon is the new mark on all densities, including the round variant, and the in-app logo uses it without a background box.
