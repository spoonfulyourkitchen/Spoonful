# Spoonful v3.5.1: an animated intro, a quieter start, and a clear note when a photo is missing

Spoonful 3.5.1 opens with a short animated welcome before sign in, animates the profile questions and the save action, replaces the marketing landing page with two plain sentences, and tells you when a recipe has no license-free photo.

## Highlights

### A welcome before sign in
The app now starts with a two-step intro. The Spoonful logo pops in and floats gently, the headline and text slide in, and the progress dots widen as you move on. The intro appears once; later starts go straight to sign in. Skip is always available at the top right. With Android's reduce-motion setting enabled, the same intro is shown without movement.

### Motion where it explains something
The goal, diet and experience questions slide in with a spring when the step changes. Saving a recipe pops the bookmark and turns it into the check mark, so the confirmation is visible before the list updates.

### Recipes without a license-free photo
Recipes without a photo, and photos that fail to load, now carry a short note that no license-free image is available. The note appears on images at least 130 px wide so small tiles stay clean, and it is translated into all 16 app languages.

### Less text on the start screen
The landing page no longer lists six feature cards, a three-step explanation, a star row or a recipe count. It shows the app name, one sentence and the button that leads to sign in. Landing copy is written for all 16 languages, and the unused marketing keys are gone.
