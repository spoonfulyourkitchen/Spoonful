# Spoonful 3.2.1

**Focus:** making things work that did not work before — and making the app feel lighter while doing it.

## Video link → recipe now actually works
Paste a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram link and Spoonful turns it into an editable recipe.

* Reads the video description **and** the subtitles, whichever contains the recipe.
* Understands ingredient lists, "Ingredients" / "Methods" sections, comma-separated lists and single run-on subtitle blocks.
* Prefers the language your app is set to, then German, then English.
* If the text is too messy for the built-in reader, the recipe is assembled once automatically — at no cost to you.
* If a video truly contains no recipe, you get the raw text with a "please review" note instead of an empty screen.
* Sharing a link from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube straight into Spoonful works every time now, not just on the first launch after installing.

## Photo cropping rebuilt
* You now see the **whole photo** while choosing the visible area.
* Drag the frame anywhere with one finger — grid and handle included.
* Pinch with two fingers to zoom in and out, like in other apps; buttons and reset are still there for precision.
* Whatever you frame is what you get: no more "I moved it to the corner and the app showed the middle".

## MONSTR is back
* A dedicated black-and-neon page for energy drink deals, reachable from the tab bar and the side menu.
* Prices are pulled from **three independent sources at the same time**; if one is unavailable, the others still deliver.
* Every source has a time limit, so "Get prices" always answers quickly instead of hanging.
* Results are compared by **price per litre**, with the best deal and the cheapest can highlighted.
* Tap a result to open the product, add it to your shopping list, or share it with your household.
* The last result is cached, so the page opens with data even before you refresh.

## A tour that explains everything
* Six new steps: AI Chef (including shared video links), Saved, Household, Community, Monstr and "Get prices".
* New users see the full tour; existing users only see the steps they have not seen yet — nobody repeats the whole tour.

## Faster, smoother, less battery
* Screen backgrounds are no longer rebuilt on every keystroke.
* Translations are cached instead of being resolved again for every label on screen.
* The tutorial highlight only redraws when the element actually moves.
* Background connection checks pause when the app is not in the foreground and refresh the moment you return.
* Timers from menus and pages are cleaned up, so nothing keeps running after you leave a screen.

## Other fixes
* Recipe tiles, search results and counters show consistent numbers.
* Version 3.2.1.
