# Spoonful v3.5.2: onboarding before sign in, a cleaner logo, and a faster library

Spoonful 3.5.2 puts the questions before the account, removes the box around the logo, drops the AI Chef description, caches the library and barcode lookups, and replaces the blocking notices with a top banner you can swipe away.

## Highlights

### Start with progress, then sign in
A new install now asks for goal, diet and cooking experience right at the start. The last step shows the plan with your answers and asks for an account so the plan stays. Answers live on the device until then, so closing the app does not throw the work away.

### Logo without the box
The logo stands on its own. The tinted background, the border and the rounded frame are gone, the mark keeps its size.

### AI Chef without the explanation
The AI Chef screen shows its title and the input, not a line of description above it.

### Library opens instantly
The first page for the current filters is stored on the device and appears right away while fresh data loads. Barcode lookups ask OpenFoodFacts directly and keep the result for 30 days, so scanning the same product twice is instant.

### Notices that stay out of the way
Notices now come as a banner at the top, slide in, hide after 3.2 seconds and can be swiped up or tapped. Errors and notices with actions stay until you close them. They no longer cover the buttons underneath.
