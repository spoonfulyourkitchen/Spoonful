# Spoonful v3.5.0: collections, a dedicated household QR scanner, and five new languages

Spoonful 3.5.0 streamlines saving recipes into collections, gives household QR joining its own scanner dialog, adds Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi and Polish, and keeps the dairy-aware AI estimates and community fixes from earlier in this release.

## Highlights

### Cleaner collection saving
The save picker no longer lists a "save without a collection" option. Tapping outside the dialog or closing it saves the recipe without a collection, while picking a collection saves it there. A long press on a collection lets you rename or delete it - deleting removes only the collection and keeps your saved recipes, after a confirmation.

### Household QR scanning with its own dialog
Joining a household by QR now opens a Spoonful-styled scanner with its own title and layout, separate from the tracker/product scanner. It scans QR codes only and offers manual code entry as a fallback.

### Six more languages
Ukrainian (Українська) is joined by Chinese (中文), Japanese (日本語), Korean (한국어), Hindi (हिन्दी) and Polish (Polski) in Settings. Main screens, the Settings and Theme screens, the household area, notifications, the tour and the legal pages are translated, theme names and taglines are localised, and anything untranslated falls back to English. AI Chef also recognises the language you write in - Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Polish and more - and answers in that language.

### More specific AI recipes and transparent estimates
AI Chef specifies dairy type and fat percentage, respects requested lactose-free ingredients, and keeps any type or fat content supplied by the cook. Nutrition estimates use standard dairy values when details are missing, show an information control with the assumptions used, and calculate with explicit fat percentages when provided.

### Community comments and reports
Community comment counts are loaded through the registered query and counted across pages. Report reasons display human-readable translated labels, including Other.
