# Spoonful 3.6.1

## Video and link recipes

- Fractions such as 1 ½ cups are read as numbers.
- Ranges such as 2-3 cloves stay intact instead of turning into 2 3.
- Sections like "For the sauce" are recognised and no longer end up in the ingredient list.
- Descriptions separated by pipes or checkbox marks are split into proper lines.
- Video and AI ingredients are merged, duplicates are dropped.

## Creating recipes

- Pasting a full recipe into the ingredients field now moves the method into the steps field automatically, so every line is no longer treated as an ingredient.

## Interface

- Video tab without the manual paste path.
- Community without the duplicate sort button under the search bar.
- Barcode sheet cleaned up: one photo button, gallery right next to it, no number field and no divider.
- Age in onboarding is a counter like height and weight.
- AI recipe opens as a popup instead of also appearing below the generate button.
- Helper descriptions under video, community, shopping list and cooking mode removed.

## Tracker and household

- The tracker starts with today's goal, progress bars and calories left, with today's meals right below. Barcode, water and the weekly review come after that.
- The household screen starts with shopping, lists, weekly plan and tasks. Members and "cooking now" sit below, expenses, board and stats stay behind "More".
- Empty household areas no longer show up as empty cards.

## Tutorial

- Starts only right after onboarding, no longer after a reinstall and a fresh sign in.
- New steps for the community feed, the AI recipe generator and the barcode scanner.

## Install

Download `Spoonful-v3.6.1.apk` below and open it on your Android phone.
