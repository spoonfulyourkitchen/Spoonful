## Spoonful 3.7.4

A smoother library, readable text in every design, and an AI chef that sticks to free models.

### New

- **My Kitchen, combined:** your own recipes and saved recipes now live in one list with clear tabs, so you always see everything you cook.
- **Pictures first:** recipes with a photo appear first in the library, across all 50-recipe pages. Nothing jumps around while loading more.
- **Correct plurals:** labels such as "1 member", "1 task" and "1 recipe" now use the right singular or plural form in all 16 languages.

### Readability

- Contrast fixed across all 20 light and dark themes: active tabs, filter counters, tracker cards, household header and recipe details.
- Proper right-to-left layout for Arabic on the web.
- Field labels no longer get clipped in Japanese, and every screen was checked in all themes and languages.

### Translations

- 694 missing strings added across Arabic, Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Polish and the other app languages.
- Cooking mode, recipe details, AI tab ingredients and the community screen now use real translations instead of English or German leftovers.

### AI Chef

- **Free models only by default:** Gemini Flash Lite, NVIDIA and OpenRouter free tiers are used first; paid models are off unless explicitly enabled.
- Recipes, nutrition estimates and answers are produced in the language chosen in the app.
- Nutrition values come from a real ingredient database and are cross-checked for consistency on every language.

### Installation

- **From 3.7.2 or newer:** install the APK over the existing app. The signature is the same and nothing is lost.
- **3.7.0 / 3.7.1 or older:** uninstall once, install 3.7.4 and sign in again. Your account data comes back from the server.
