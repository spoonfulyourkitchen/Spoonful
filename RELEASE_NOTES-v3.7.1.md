# Spoonful v3.7.1

## Highlights

- **A large product database.** Spoonful now carries 1.5 million products, including the private labels of Edeka, Lidl, Rewe, Aldi, Kaufland, Penny, Netto, Norma, tegut, Alnatura, denn's, dm, Rossmann, Globus, Marktkauf, Hit and Aro. Barcode scans and product searches are answered from the database first and only fall back to the live service when a code is still unknown.
- **Product search is much faster.** Typical searches answer in 6 to 15 ms instead of seconds, with prefix matches first and brands as a second pass.
- **Recent foods in meal logging.** "Log meal" has a third page with the eight foods you logged most often in the last 14 days, one tap adds them with the usual portion.

## Notes

- No database migration is required on the device.
- Products that are still missing are fetched live and stored for the next lookup.
- The local-only test button remains limited to localhost development builds.
