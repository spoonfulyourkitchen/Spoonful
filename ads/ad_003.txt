SPOONFUL AD #3: Money Saver
===========================

STYLE: problem_solution
DURATION: 18 seconds
TARGET AUDIENCE: Budget-conscious families

HOOK (3s):
"Throwing away spoiled food?"

PROBLEM (4s):
"Buying ingredients you don't use?
Money literally in the trash can?"

SOLUTION (6s):
"Save recipes, track what you have,
shop smarter with Spoonful.
Your shelf + calorie tracker help you use what you buy."

KEY FEATURE (3s):
Your shelf + calorie tracking

CTA (2s):
"Stop wasting food money"

VISUALS:
- Trash can with food items falling out
- Fridge with expired food visible
- Phone showing Spoonful "Your Shelf" page
- Recipe cards being saved
- Progress bar filling (calorie tracker)
- Shopping list with checked items
- Family sitting down to happy meal

MUSIC: Starts slightly sad, turns positive and empowering
VOICEOVER: Caring, solution-oriented
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Stop wasting food. Start saving money."
